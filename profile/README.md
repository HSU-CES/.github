# Computer Engineering Scientific Association

**Hakim Sabzevari University (HSU)**

Welcome to the official GitHub organization of the Computer Engineering Scientific Association at Hakim Sabzevari University.

We develop and maintain digital platforms, software projects, and open-source resources to support our academic community.

## Our Activities

- Developing and maintaining the association's official website
- Building digital tools for academic and student activities
- Supporting collaborative software development
- Sharing educational resources and open-source projects

## Get Involved

We welcome students and contributors interested in software development, design, and technology.

Explore our repositories and contribute to our projects.
